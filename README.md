# HR-Analysis-Dashboard
1. **Project Title
HR Analytics Dashboard** – Employee Insights and Attrition Analysis

**2. Short Description**

The HR Analytics Dashboard provides insights into workforce composition, employee attrition, salary distribution, demographics, and employee trends. It helps HR teams understand workforce patterns and identify factors that may influence employee turnover.
The dashboard presents key HR metrics through interactive visualizations, enabling users to explore employee data and make informed workforce management decisions.

**3. Tech Stack**

**Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization. 

**Microsoft Excel:** Data preparation, organization, and preliminary analysis.

**4. Data Source**
The raw dataset used for this project was acquired from _Maven Analytics_. The data was used to perform analysis, identify trends, evaluate key performance indicators (KPIs), and develop interactive visualizations in Power BI.

**5. Features / Highlights**

Employee headcount and workforce overview.
Employee attrition analysis and attrition rate monitoring.
Workforce composition by department and other employee categories.
Salary distribution and compensation analysis.
Employee demographic analysis.
Exploration of employee trends and turnover patterns.
Interactive charts and filters for analyzing different workforce segments.

**6. Business Problem / Solutions**

**Business Problem:**

Organizations need to understand employee turnover, workforce composition, and salary patterns to improve employee retention and workforce planning. Analyzing large employee datasets manually can make it difficult to identify important trends and potential areas of concern.

**Solution:**

The HR Analytics Dashboard consolidates employee information into an interactive reporting interface. It helps HR professionals monitor attrition, examine workforce demographics, understand salary distribution, and identify employee groups that may require further investigation.

**Business Value:**

Supports data-driven HR decisions.
Helps identify workforce and attrition patterns.
Improves visibility into employee-related KPIs.
Supports employee retention and workforce planning discussions.
